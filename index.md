I am Mainak, a Senior Project Assistant at the [Medical Imaging and Theranostics Laboratory](https://mitlabiitkgp.in), School of Medical Science and Technology, [IIT Kharagpur](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/) where I work on developing efficient and robust segmentation techniques for effective modeling of pediatric heart under [Prof. Subhamoy Mandal](https://sites.google.com/site/smandalbiomed/home). 

Over the past few years, I walked my first baby steps as a researcher owing to some wonderful people and collaborations. Most recently, I was as a Senior Project Associate at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, [IIT Kanpur](https://www.iitk.ac.in) under [Prof. Amitansghu Pal](https://www.iitk.ac.in/amitangshu-pal) where I worked on developing diagnostic evaluation framework for Deep-Learning-based anomaly detectors. Before that, I was a Junior Project Engineer and Project Assistant at the [Complex Network Research Group (CNeRG) Lab](https://cnerg-iitkgp.github.io/), Department of Computer Science Engineering, [IIT Kharagpur](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/) where I worked on adapting vocabulary of Language Models for low-resource languages and building end-to-end pipelines for effective cross-lingual summarization of long Indian legal judgments under [Prof. Saptarshi Ghosh](https://sites.google.com/site/saptarshighosh/). Broadly, I am interested in making Deep Learning and Language Models trustworthy, reliable and efficient for high-stakes domains. I am also interested in robustness and analysis.

Before that, I was a Research Intern at the Futuristic Wireless and Network Technologies Group, G.S. Sanyal School of Telecommunications, [IIT Kharagpur](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/), where I worked with [Prof. Debarati Sen](https://sites.google.com/view/debarati-sen) on designing modern digital communication systems by fine-tuning Deep Neural Networks.

**Keywords:** Language Models, Cross-lingual Summarization, Alignment, Deep Neural Networks

In the past, I was fortunate to intern at the [IIT (ISM) Dhanbad](https://www.iitism.ac.in/) under [Prof. Samrat Mukhopadhyay](https://sites.google.com/view/samratspace/home?authuser=0), focusing on implementation and testing on Generalized Online Caching Algorithms. I also has an amazing time at [Suvidha Foundation](https://suvidhafoundationedutech.org/) where I developed algorithms for automatic detection of plant species using deep Convolutional networks.
